I am co-organizing a Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) 2026 workshop! The workshop is [**Do Robots Need World Models? A Debate on Dynamics Learning, Planning, and End-to-End Robot Learning**](https://do-robots-need-world-models.github.io/). This workshop aims to foster spirited debates around whether (and in what form) future robots require world models to interact with the environment.

This workshop was the brainchild of [Alberta Longhini](https://albilo17.github.io/), [Wenlong Huang](https://wenlonghuang.com/), [Bardienus Duisterhof](https://www.bart-ai.com/), and [Lasse Peters](https://lasse-peters.net/). Additional organization support was provided by [Siddharth Patki](https://sidpatki.github.io/), [Jeffrey Ichnowski](https://ichnow.ski/), and [Fei-Fei Li](https://svl.stanford.edu/).
