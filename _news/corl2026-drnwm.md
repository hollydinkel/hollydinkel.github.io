I am co-organizing a Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) 2026 workshop! The workshop is [**Do Robots Need World Models? A Debate on Dynamics Learning, Planning, and End-to-End Robot Learning**](https://do-robots-need-world-models.github.io/). This workshop aims to build connections among top talent and leaders in robotics and AI to develop the academic and industry ecosystem around robotic interaction with non-rigid objects.

Thank you in particular to [Alberta Longhini](https://albilo17.github.io/) and [Wenlong Huang](https://wenlonghuang.com/) for their heroic organizational efforts, along with [Bardienus Duisterhof](https://www.bart-ai.com/), [Lasse Peters](https://lasse-peters.net/), [Jeffrey Ichnowski](https://ichnow.ski/), and [Fei-Fei Li](https://svl.stanford.edu/), for making this event so great!
